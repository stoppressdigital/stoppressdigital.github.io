The Challenge

A fragrance brand with real ambition, no identity, and a market that doesn't forgive a weak first impression.

Before StopPress came in, the business was tracking around 70 orders a month — roughly what the data shows through the first half of 2025. No brand system, no consistent marketing, no infrastructure to grow from. The challenge was to build a brand that could hold its own against established DTC players, then run marketing that compounds over time. Everything had to be right from launch because in DTC, there's no second first impression.

The Trajectory

H1 2025 averaged 71.5 orders per month. By H2 2025 — as the brand identity bedded in and the marketing found its rhythm — that number climbed to an average of 124 per month. 2026 entered with that momentum already built. January opened at 143. The first four months of 2026 totalled 534 orders, 92% ahead of the same period in 2025. Full-year 2026 is projecting approximately 1,900 orders, roughly 60% above Year One.
